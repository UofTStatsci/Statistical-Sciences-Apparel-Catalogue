
# Statistical Sciences Apparel Catalogue

University of Toronto  
Department of Statistical Sciences

## About

The Statistical Sciences Apparel Catalogue is an online platform for members of the University of Toronto Department of Statistical Sciences to browse departmental apparel and submit requests for items they are interested in purchasing.

The website allows members to select apparel styles, colours, sizes and quantities, and generate an itemized request that can be emailed to the department.

The catalogue does not process payments or display prices. Pricing and availability will be confirmed following a quotation from the University of Toronto Bookstore.

Submitting an apparel request does not constitute a purchase or commitment to buy.

## Features

- Responsive, UofT-branded catalogue
- Introductory splash screen featuring the official Statistical Sciences seal
- Product images and descriptions
- Colour and size selection
- Quantity selection
- Shopping cart with editable quantities
- Itemized email request generation
- Copy-to-clipboard alternative
- No payment processing or database required

## Technology

The website uses:

- HTML5

## Order Request Process

1. Browse the apparel catalogue.
2. Select products, colours, sizes and quantities.
3. Add selections to the request cart.
4. Review the complete request.
5. Enter contact information.
6. Generate an itemized email addressed to the department.
7. Send the email using your preferred email application.

The department will review requests and obtain a quotation from the University of Toronto Bookstore.

## Configuration

The destination email address and product catalogue are configured in `js/app.js`.

Products and images can be added or updated without changing the overall website structure.

## Branding
