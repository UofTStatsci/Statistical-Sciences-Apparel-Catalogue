Your Information

Submit Your Request

Please review all selected products, colours, sizes and quantities before submitting your apparel request.

Prices are estimates only. The department will confirm final pricing and availability before any purchase is arranged.

Complete your submission

  1. Select Submit Apparel Request below.
  2. Your pre-populated Microsoft Forms checkout will open on this website.
  3. Sign in with your University of Toronto account if prompted.
  4. Review your information and click Submit within Microsoft Forms.

Your request is not submitted until you complete the final submission in Microsoft Forms.

Following successful submission, you should receive an automated confirmation email containing your apparel request.

If you experience difficulties with the checkout, use the alternative form link or select Copy Request Details.

Apparel requests are processed through Microsoft Forms. Your information is transferred using a prefilled link and may remain in browser history or logs. Please do not include sensitive personal information in the notes field.
